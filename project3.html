Royal City (Ullal)

BMICAPA Approved Individual 'E' Katha sites

The Vision

Golden Grain Farms represents a unique synergy between high-yield agricultural potential and strategic land subdivision. This 40-plot community was designed for those seeking to invest in fertile soil with pre-installed modern farming infrastructure.

Project Specifications

Location Ullal, KA
Inventory plots starting from ₹1.06 Crore*
Land Profile High-fertility topsoil suitable for horticulture and cash crops
Architecture Strategic land subdivision with standardized boundary markers

Infrastructure & Engineering

Irrigation

Sustainable borehole systems connected to a centralized drip-line network for water efficiency.

Connectivity

12-meter wide internal compacted gravel roads designed for agricultural heavy machinery.

Sustainability

Integrated greywater harvesting and communal compost processing zones.

Security

Reinforced perimeter fencing with a central gated monitoring station at Dasarahalli Ext. entrance.

Strategic Location Benefits

Situated in the Dasarahalli Extension, the project offers the perfect balance of rural fertility and accessibility to the urban distribution centers, ensuring that the 40 plot owners have a direct pipeline for their agricultural produce.
