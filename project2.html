Architectural Fusion

Serene Valley Villas represents a dialogue between the Brutalist movement—characterized by raw, exposed concrete and bold geometric forms—and Organic architecture, which seeks to integrate the building into the natural topography of Mysore Road's rolling landscape.

Each villa is positioned to respect the natural watershed of the 12.5-acre estate, ensuring that the heavy stone structures feel light amidst the lush greenery.
