Due Diligence

Beyond the Surface.

At Apti Realty, we don't just look at a plot; we analyze its potential. Our comprehensive process ensures every site is viable for your specific needs—from high-rise construction to long-term investment holding.

Physical Site Visits

On-ground inspections to verify boundaries, topography, and structural feasibility.

Key Checks:
  • Soil Health & Stability
  • Groundwater Depth
  • Existing Structures

Market Value Analysis

Data-driven valuations based on current demand, historical trends, and liquidity.

Growth Factors:
  • Historical Price Trends
  • Urban Proximity Index
  • Upcoming Infra Projects

Zone Verification

Alignment checks with government master plans and future urban regulations.

Legal Verification:
  • Land Use Classification
  • FSI/FAR Calculations
  • Buffer Zone Checks

Urban Growth & Connectivity Audit

Location is non-negotiable. Our audits include a Proximity Report, detailing access to highways, transit hubs, and healthcare. We calculate the "Urban Shift" to predict how soon the city's expansion will reach your site, maximizing your future ROI.
