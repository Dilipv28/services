City Lights. Mountain Silence.

Tranquil Heights is a master-planned hillside enclave designed for those who seek the duality of urban proximity and peak serenity. Spread across a naturally contoured landscape, each of the 25 villas is tiered to ensure unobstructed, 180-degree panoramic views of the city skyline.

The architecture utilizes "Earth-Sheltered" principles, where the structures work with the hillside rather than against it. This reduces the carbon footprint and provides natural thermal insulation, keeping interiors cool throughout the year.
