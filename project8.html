A Vertical Statement of Ambition

Skyline Residences is a 40,000 Sq.Ft masterstroke in urban high-rise design. Situated on the iconic MG Road, the project is engineered to serve as a landmark of modern luxury. The architectural language focuses on structural transparency—blending expansive glass facades with reinforced steel to create a silhouette that is both powerful and weightless.

The planning phase prioritizes a "Stacked Lifestyle" model: ground-level high-street retail vibrancy, mid-tier corporate exclusivity, and upper-tier residential sanctuary, all culminating in a world-class leisure deck.
