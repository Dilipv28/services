Harmony Hills

Where Architecture Breathes

Bannerghatta Road
30 Managed Villas
Biophilic Modernism

Harmony Hills is an intentional residential enclave designed to restore the connection between the modern dweller and the natural world. Positioned within the lush surroundings of the Bannerghatta Road corridor, the project features 30 bespoke villas that utilize "Light-Touch" architectural techniques to preserve the existing topography.

The community is anchored by a series of interconnected communal gardens, designed by leading landscape architects to serve as functional outdoor living rooms. By prioritizing shared green spaces over high-density construction, Harmony Hills offers a rare low-density lifestyle in one of South Bengaluru's most sought-after belts.

Edible Forest Trails

Communal orchards and herb gardens for resident use.

Natural Ventilation

Villas designed to capture the valley breeze, reducing AC dependency.

Zen Water Features

Recycled water systems powering aesthetic koi ponds and brooks.

Social Courtyards

Centralized gathering spots for community interaction.
