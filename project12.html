Modernism Meets Metaphorical Nature

EcoVista Complex is a 50,000 Sq.Ft mixed-use intervention designed to introduce a high-performance "green lung" into the heart of HSR Layout. The architectural strategy utilizes vertical forests and integrated photovoltaic skins to blur the lines between structure and landscape.

The development is strategically zoned to balance the commercial energy of the ground-tier retail concourse with the high-seclusion residential suites on the upper levels. This results in a self-sustaining micro-community that prioritizes human wellness and architectural transparency.

Retail Tier

Luxury boutique frontages with semi-open pedestrian plazas.

Green Core

Central light-well with vertical air-purification gardens.

Residential

Smart-integrated units with panoramic park views.

Renewables

Hybrid solar-wind facades providing 30% of common area power.
