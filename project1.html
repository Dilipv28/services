Architectural Concept

The Obsidian Plaza stands as a testament to 21st-century commercial design. The structure is defined by its Modernist glass-facade, engineered to provide panoramic views of Bengaluru’s skyline while maintaining high thermal efficiency.

Designed specifically for high-growth tech headquarters, the building utilizes an open-plan structural grid, allowing for maximum flexibility in workspace configuration. The "Obsidian" moniker is reflected in the use of dark-tinted reflective glazing and matte black aluminum mullions.

  • ✦ Sustainable Envelope: High-performance Low-E glass reduces solar heat gain by 40%.
  • ✦ Structural Fluidity: Column-free floor plates supported by a central core.
  • ✦ Smart Integration: Fully automated HVAC and lighting systems optimized for the IT sector.
