RV Harmony Hub

An integrated architectural ecosystem redefining the "Live-Work-Play" paradigm through biophilic design and adaptive mixed-use planning.

Executive Summary

The Urban Oasis Complex is a signature 30,000 Sq.Ft development positioned at the nexus of Whitefield’s commercial district. By integrating Grade-A office suites with boutique retail and luxury residential tiers, the project solves for the modern professional’s need for proximity and wellness.

Total Site Footprint 30,000 Sq.Ft
Green Coverage 40% Verticality
Zoning Mixed-Use (C-2)
Est. Completion Q3 2028

Architectural Pillars

Biophilic Integration

Cascading rooftop gardens and internal "green lungs" that naturally regulate building temperature.

Mixed-Use Fluidity

Separated circulation paths for corporate tenants and residential occupants to ensure total privacy.

Sustainable Core

High-performance glazing and solar-integrated facades targeting LEED Gold certification.

Smart Logistics

AI-managed basement parking with EV charging stations for all residential units.

Component Breakdown

  • Retail (Lvl 0-1): High-street luxury frontage.
  • Corporate (Lvl 2-4): Adaptive modular floorplates.
  • Residential (Lvl 5+): Limited edition sky-villas.
  • Amenities: Infinity pool and glass-walled gym.
