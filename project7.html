The Blueprint for Better Living

Green Acres is a 50-plot master-planned community designed to bridge the gap between rural tranquility and suburban convenience. Located in the rapidly appreciating Hoskote region, this project moves away from standard plot layouts to offer a curated environment with integrated sustainable systems.

Every plot is positioned to maximize airflow and natural light, while the shared community infrastructure is designed with a "zero-impact" philosophy, ensuring that the development enhances the local ecology rather than disrupting it.

Eco-Friendly Infrastructure

Water Management

Centralized rainwater harvesting and permeable paving for groundwater recharge.

Community Spaces

2.5 acres of dedicated green parks, organic orchards, and walking trails.

Solar Power

All street lighting and common area utilities powered by off-grid solar arrays.

Smart Utilities

Underground electrical cabling and fiber-to-the-plot connectivity infrastructure.
