The Ecosystem

Completed in late 2024, **Tech Hub Tower** stands as the premier destination for high-growth startups and established tech giants. The architectural mission was to create a "frictionless" workspace—utilizing high-performance glass facades and open-core engineering to allow maximum floorplate flexibility.

From the 10,000 sq.ft co-working mezzanine to the specialized hardware innovation labs on the upper floors, every square inch is optimized for 24/7 technical operations.
