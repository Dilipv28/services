Agile Workspace Engineering

Innovation Park is a 70,000 Sq.Ft commercial benchmark designed to support the rapid scaling of modern tech organizations. Completed in 2023, the development prioritizes Structural Fluidity—allowing tenants to reconfigure floor plates based on evolving team sizes and collaboration needs.

Located in the high-density Whitefield corridor, the project serves as a "Plug-and-Play" ecosystem. From hyper-fast 10Gbps networking to dedicated hardware prototyping zones, the building is architecturally optimized for 24/7 technical performance.
